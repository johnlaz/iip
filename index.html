Read the market. Value the company. Track what you own.

Three linked apps that share one dataset — macro regime, company valuation and your portfolio. It runs in your browser with your own keys: no backend, no account, no monthly bill.

Launch IIP → How it works

Installs like a native app · works offline for the shell · your data stays on your device

Three apps, one dataset

Set a key in any of them and it is live in all of them. Look up a stock in Research and Portfolio already knows its margin of safety.

Macro

A six-pillar regime score built from percentile math you can inspect, sector rotation calls, and a backtest that only uses what was known at the time.

Research

DCF, modified Graham and historical multiples side by side — a range, not a false-precision number — plus an S&P 500 screener and AI earnings-call analysis.

Portfolio

What you own, how far it has drifted from target, plain overweight / underweight flags, and a journal for the reasoning behind each call.

Up and running in three steps

No sign-up. Everything is stored in your browser.

Open the app

Launch IIP and use the install prompt to add it to your home screen or dock.

Add your keys

A free Financial Modeling Prep key powers the data. An optional free Groq key adds AI briefings and call analysis.

Explore

Start in Macro for the regime, drill into a company in Research, then see it land in Portfolio.

Honest limitations. Valuation math is verified against synthetic data and is still being sanity-checked on real companies. The screener uses trailing ratios, not the full valuation engine. IIP is a tool for your own research — not investment advice and not a licensed financial product.
