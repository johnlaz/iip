What is this page?

Each pillar on the Regime tab already has a score, worked out purely from data — no opinions involved. This page lets you decide how much each pillar should count when they're combined into one number. Push a slider up and that pillar counts for more; push it down and it counts for less. There's no correct setting — professional economists weight these differently too. Move a slider and watch the score change to see how much any one pillar is actually driving it.
